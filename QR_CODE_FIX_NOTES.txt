QR Code fix verification

This patch keeps the two QR code flows separate:

1. Office address/location QR codes
   - File: backend/app/api/attendance.py
   - Purpose: staff scan these to sign in / sign out.
   - This patch does NOT change that logic.

2. Staff ID card QR codes
   - File: backend/app/api/franchise_staff.py
   - Purpose: QR printed/displayed on employee and manager staff ID cards.
   - Target priority:
     a) franchise_users.website
     b) Google Maps link for office_address / office_address_assigned
     c) https://martinsdirect.com fallback

Verified in backend/app/api/franchise_staff.py:
- _staff_qr_target() is used for digital ID cards.
- _staff_qr_target_for_pdf() is used for PDF staff ID cards.
- _id_card_staff_rows() selects fu.website from franchise_users.
- export_id_cards supports staff_type + staff_id so single selected card can be generated.

After applying:
1. Make sure franchise_users.website has the correct website address.
2. Restart backend.
3. Download one staff ID card and scan the QR.
4. It should open the franchise website, not the office attendance QR.
