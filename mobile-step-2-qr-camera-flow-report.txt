Mobile Step 2 QR camera flow update

Updated file:
frontend/src/pages/MobileAttendancePage.jsx

Behavior now:
- Step 2 opens the live QR scanner as the default path.
- The scanner requests the mobile rear camera.
- When a QR code is detected, it is validated/registered immediately.
- After successful registration, the attendance flow moves automatically to Step 3.
- Manual four-digit code entry is not shown at the start of Step 2.
- Manual entry becomes available only if:
  - the browser does not support automatic QR detection,
  - camera permission/camera startup fails, or
  - the rear camera stays open for 45 seconds without detecting a QR code.
- The manual fallback text tells the employee to get the four-digit code from a manager/franchise user.
