# Version 3.1 phased enhancements

1. Added Joinings as a standard fixed-quantity commission for every franchise, employee and manager.
2. Hardened self-service quantity commission submission, calculation, pending status, and notifications to linked manager plus franchise user.
3. Password reset now resolves login/profile email for all active user roles. SMTP environment variables are still required on Render.
4. Master account retains franchise user visibility through HR Staff / franchise administration.
5. Employee edits remain partial updates; omitted ID, username and password values are preserved.
6. Business Information is visible to master and franchise accounts.
7. Additional office addresses remain supported and assignable to staff.
8. Mobile portrait staff ID styling now preserves the landscape card proportions and layout.
9. QR is optional; GPS and signature remain required. Outside-area attendance is flagged and notified.
10. Successful QR scans automatically capture and store a camera image with the attendance event.
